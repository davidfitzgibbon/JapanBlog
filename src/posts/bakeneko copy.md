I've been getting a coding newsletter for a few years. Each week there's a coding challenge and a few of us reply to the organiser with our solutions. The next week she shared all our solutions and we can all see different ways of coding the same thing. She even includes a terrible joke, usually a pun. It's great!

I happened to mention that I was in Japan in last weeks' one, and one of the guys replied. He also sends in his code a lot, so I've seen him online regularly, and he's always seemed nice. Well he was on holidays here last week and we met up!

{% fig "/img/w6/w6-ten.png" "Ten and I trying the delights of CoCo Curry" %}

We went to CoCo Curry. It's a local chain. I'd never tried it so was excited, and Ten said that it's in Singapore (where he's from), but that there were options in Japan that weren't at home.

As you can see from the empty plates above, we liked it! It was fairly basic, as you might expect for a chain. But the meat was so juicy it was _insane_. Fast-food has no business being that tasty.

### Fake Quake

Not sure if the news made it outside of Japan, but there was an earthquake warning here. Every phone in Japan blared the warning. It was telling us that a force 9.9 quake had hit north Hokkaido.

I was in a Lounge with two students (like a non-structured class where we chat or play games in English). They stayed super cool. I looked for a responsible adult to see if we should start running or praying.

Turns out it was all a sensor issue, and in reality nothing had happened. So all was well.

It's been interesting feeling the odd gentle quake. Fascinating to get used to them at night while you sleep. But when the real deal alarm goes off, it's fully scary. Glad it was all nothing, and hoping that's the last time I hear that alarm.

### Hakuraku Street Festival

We were getting observed this week to make sure we were settling in well. Of course we passed with flying colors, but we were still pretty tuckered from making sure that we did well.

{% video "/img/w6/w6-festival-1.mp4" "Walking through the arcade towards the music" %}

With that in mind, we only really left the house on Satruday to hit Hakuraku for their (I think unoffical) Street Festival. Some here call it other things like "The Black Market". I've tried to Google it, and cant find a name.

{% video "/img/w6/w6-festival-2.mp4" "It was all pretty jazzy / arty stuff, till this lad started wailing!" %}

Anywho, it's a street very near Eni's campus. They take the whole street and have a party. Shops are selling special snacks from their doors, carparks had bands playing in them, people drinking through the streets. All a tiny bit loose (by Japanese standards), but a good night had.
