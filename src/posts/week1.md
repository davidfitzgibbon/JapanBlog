So it's Sunday night, 5 days into the adventure to Japan. We're pretty tired, but happy, excited and looking forward to what's next.

But lets rewind a little.

### DAY 1

The trip over was fine. The usual experience of flying for almost 24 hours straight. What changed though was when we landed.

We were met by some of the team that we'll be working with. They were meeting to help get us set up. They had our phones with phone numbers already set up, keys to our apartment, info on living in Machida and helpful tips on how to get to our apartment!

It was a bit of an ordeal getting our bags to the apartment. Transferring lines, in Shinjuku, at 6pm, when we were already exhausted, wasn't the best experience, but we got it done!

The apartment itself is great. It's bigger than we expected. When we got there we found we'd had bed clothes and kitchen/bathroom essentials delivered for us.

It was a really great welcome, and a good sign that the company was really looking after us.

### DAY 2

After not quite enough sleep we had to get straight up to meet more people from the company who were to help us get our affairs set up with the local government and the bank. Thank God they were there to help us. There were many different documents and queues for different things, and everything was in Japanese. Our colleagues stayed with us for the 3 - 4 hours it took to get everything sorted out. We were still jetlagged but it really felt like we'd gotten a big important job done.

### DAY 3

Our first day of training!

We got to meet the whole team. Everyone seems really nice and enthusiastic. Also, there are a lot of people returning, which is a good sign. The senior staff also seem really approachable and know their stuff. Feels like we'll be well looked after.

After our first day, and given it was a Friday, we also got a few drinks and brought them home, watching a few shows from home to chill out.

### DAY 4

A very quiet day. We were mainly trying to catch up on sleep and get to know the area.

### DAY 5

That's today! We went out to find some furnature for the apartment and other little bits we needed. We did really well, finding almost everything we needed.

More importantly, it felt like we could really starting doing what we wanted, rather than admin related to living and working here.

The big success of the day was finding a place that does a mapo tofu for Eni!

---

This entry might not have been the most thrillingly written, but we're still pretty broken! Will hopefully have a little more energy for it next week!
