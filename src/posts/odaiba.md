We were really into full work mode this week. One thing I was appreciating this week in the salary man lifestyle was how polite everyone is, to a fault.

{% fig "/img/w4-polite.jpg" "A whole train of people queing to go up the right of the stairs, while only one person walks down." %}

I got the train from Yokohama at about 5:30, and this is us getting off in Machida at about 6:30. Peak rush hour. Despite that, not a soul would dream of sneaking to the left and getting ahead.

### Weekend Time

We finished off the week by going to a pizza place with some homies from work. It's a pizza place in Yokohama called Shakeys. They do all you can eat pizza, and all you can drink gin and beer, for two hours for ¥2,855. Or about €15. Needless to say we enjoyed ourselves there for two hours then screamed for another two hours at the nearest karaoke bar!

### Feeling Shakey

The Shakey's experience necessarily left us feeling shakey.

Part of the solution here was to try a local "delicacy". 7-Eleven do a "pizza bun". A kinda bao / pizza hybrid.

{% fig "/img/w4-pizza-bun.jpg" "The soft smiling face of the 7-Eleven pizza bun!" %}

I've got to say, it was hard to find this little buddy. And there had been a decent amount of build up. I'm sad to say though that it felt very processed, and not very tasty. Still fun to have tried it out.

While out and about, I also happened across this thing.

{% fig "/img/w4-bug-caterpillar.jpg" "Is it from a bracelet?" %}

I honestly thought it had fallen off a bracelet or something... until it moved.

{% fig "/img/w4-bug-hawk-caterpillar.jpg" "Hawk Moth Caterpillar - not my photo" %}

Turns out it was a more adult version of these fellas. They start out green, go dark, then grow. The one I saw was as long as my middle finger, and about as thick.

It turns out I'd met their older versions during the week too! I got bumped into the biggest moth I've ever seen.

{% fig "/img/w4-bug-hawk-moth.jpg" "Hawk Moth - not my photo" %}

I got hit in the chest by one of these things. Honestly, they so big it's crazy. Just look at that photo! They're bigger than a robin or other little Irish birds like that.

Crazy!

### Local Indian

We had sampled the local Indian takeaway and really liked it. We found out that it's actually about three blocks away and felt guilty about getting it delivered, so went down to try it ourselves.

It blew my mind.

We were greeted with a lovely lettuce, cabbage and cucumber salad with a delicate masala over the top.

{% fig "/img/w4-indian-1.jpg" "Appetiser" %}

Then for the mains I had a Jeera Rice, Baigan Chicken, a popadom, a beer and a Tandori checken leg I forgot to photograph.

{% fig "/img/w4-indian-2.jpg" "Jeera Rice - phenom!" %}

The Jeera Rice (basically cumin flavor rice, plus a few other falvors) was good enough on it's own I'd have been happy with just that!

### Super Sunday

We were feeling much more chipper on Sunday. We went out to grab a few bits we were missing, like mote clothes hangers and the like.

On the way we went to Don Quixote (kinda a Lidl, but for everything in your life, from groceries, to costmetics, to clothes and electronics).

{% fig "/img/w4-insects.jpg" "Which would you try first?" %}

Came across these delights. "Ready-to-eat" they might be, I was not.

Instead of eating bugs, we went to a local bakery that we'd been recommended.

It was pretty expensive, esentially Irish prices, but the quality was great.

{% fig "/img/w4-machida-bakery.jpg" "Avocado and bacon salad" %}

It was a lovely fresh salad, avocado, bacon, an egg, a cup of tomato soup, and an iced tea. That tiny little golden jug had just a little sweet syrup in it to cut across the strong tea. All delightful!

### Odaiba

That evening we zipped out to Odaiba for the sea lantern festival.

We'd never been out that way before, so werent really sure what we were going to find. We knew we wanted to see the lanterns, there's a Statue of Liberty out there, and I'd seen a go-kart track there too.

We then tried to hot-foot it to the karting before the sun went down for the lights.

Unfortunately, it was booked out till 5pm. It looked like the name was "Tom's", along with some of the names on the timing boards. There were also some fancy cars outside.

{% fig "/img/w4-karting.jpg" "Ferrari and fancy Toyota. There was a Porsche and a Ferrari FXX too!" %}

My theory is that this was the Tom's Racing Team on a day out.

We ended up having some lovely ramen in a shopping mall that was kinda ship themed in the meantime. Zipped back to karting, and then went to the lights. We were in such a rush, and had to pop things into lockers that we got no photos of the karting. I'm kinda delighted, just means we'll have to go back!

{% fig "/img/w4-odaiba-01.jpg" "Swirls of lights" %}

The lights were amazing. Thousands of paper lanterns on the beach, in all kinda of patterns. Some were 20-30 meter wave patterns or fish. Some were hearts just big enough for a person to sit in, down to just a few in a circle. There must have been about 500m of these pieces of art. It was really amazing with the ships in the bay, and the rest of Tokyo in the background.

{% fig "/img/w4-odaiba-02.jpg" "Captain Chins" %}

It was another succesful weekend, and another tough Monday after all the craic!
