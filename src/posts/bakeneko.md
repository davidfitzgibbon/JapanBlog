### Kaiten Sushi

When we were training this year, the experienced teachers told us we could move the students by mentioning "kaiten sushi". That's the conveyor belt style sushi, and by mentioning it, the students would rotate themselves around a row of seats, giving themselves new partners.

All that said, we'd never tried real kaiten sushi, till Saturday!

{% fig "/img/w5-sushi2.jpg" "Our counter, with the conveyor of tasty treats!" %}

The sushi was all very nice. They even had a smokey / seared wagyu beef sushi. That was impressive on the menu, but less so in the mouth.

The triple tuna was amazing though! Essentially it's three different cuts of the fish, each with varying amounts of fat. Fascinating to try, but I gotta say, the basic zero fat tuna was the nicest to me.

{% fig "/img/w5-sushi3.jpg" "Blue Fin Tuna x3 ways" %}

Because you're just pulling bits off the conveyor (you can order too, in fact that's what we mostly did), there's no one keeping a full tally of your spending. Except your plates!

{% fig "/img/w5-sushi1.jpg" "Pricing chart, by plate color" %}

You end up with a stack of plates, and each plate has a different cost. The waitress we had did some kinda voodoo to get the plates all in cost order, then worked out the price in her head! Crazy considering we had about 15 plates, mostly with different prices, and two beers.

{% fig "/img/w5-sushi4.jpg" 'Our "bill"' %}

All in all it came out to a little under €30 for all of that! We will definitely be going back!

### Bakeneko Festival

On Sunday we went to Kagurazaka for the Bakeneko Festival (Ghost Cat Festival).

It's a really nice old-school part of town. Slightly less built up, less commercial. And every year they have a ghost cat festival in honor of the author Natsume Sōseki and his book "I am a Cat".

{% fig "/img/w5-bake1.jpg" "People dressed their cats up." %}

It was really chilled. People dressed up as cats. People dressed their cats up as people. There was a parade that happened a few times through the day. Each time it had a different theme and went about 500m up and down the street.

{% fig "/img/w5-bake2.jpg" "A person, dressed as a bear, who's dressed up as a cat" %}

There was a treasure hunt through the town, really as an excuse to get people to see all the parks, temples and things around.

{% fig "/img/w5-bake3.jpg" "Us meeting Cindy The Clown, one of the clown/cat street performers" %}

Everything was very calm and family friendly. There was even a crew of kids with a big float like they'd have for a temple in the shape of a cat.

{% fig "/img/w5-bake4.jpg" "Eni have greetings with teh fanciest of felines" %}

And last but not least, Captain Cat Sparrow. It was incredible the amount of cats in strollers, and they were just sitting there happy out. I never thought it was possible to have a cat stay still like that!

{% fig "/img/w5-bake5.jpg" "Our vote for best costume, Captain Cat Sparrow" %}
