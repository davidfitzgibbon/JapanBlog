It seems like much more than nine days since the last one of these!

### Training continues

Monday to Wednesday we continued our training with work. The first campus we were training on at Hakuraku is where Eni will be spending her whole time working. I'll only be there on a Tuesday morning for one class.

On Thursday, I went to the Minatomirai campus. It's a new part of town, built on reclaimed land, to be a town of the future. Minatomirai litterally translates as "Port Future". It's all huge buildings and wide avenues, and now the main center for shopping and tourism in Yokohama.

The campus building is really modern, only about 5 years old. A fun thing for me is that it's surrounded by a RollsRoyce garage and not one but TWO Porsche garages. Glorious!

{% fig "/img/week2-campus1.jpeg" "Nice bright minimal foyer" %}

The foyer of the campus is really nice and bright. There are various shops with stationary supplies, a cafe, some workshops, and I even have an open session there on a Friday morning where students, or even the public, can join to learn some English.

{% fig "/img/week2-campus2.jpeg" "7th floor terrace" %}

Several of the floors have really nice terraces, often attached to a cafe or restaurant.

{% fig "/img/week2-campus3.jpeg" "View from the top" %}

And the canteen on the 21st floor is said to even have views of Mt. Fuji on a clear day. I _think_ that's the start of Fuji on the right of the picture there.

### First day of school

Friday was our first day of school proper. And the first day the students would be back after the summer break. Silver week is a little late this year, so we all have Mon -> Wed off, so not too many students turned up on the Friday before a x5 day weekend.

I had one student turn up to a session before lunch, and one after. It was really nice to meet them and have some chats. They seemed really nice, full of excitement, and already amazing at English!

### School's out

As is tradition, a few of the teachers invited us out for drinks after our first week of school. It was lots of fun getting to know the new homies. They're from all over, but mainly it seems to be US, UK, Canada, Ireland. Lots of fun had, BUT I misread the train timetable, so we had to get a taxi home. €80 quid we hadnt intended on spending, but was glad to get home!

### Silver week

So then we had five days off! What would we get up to? What oriental adventures could we conjure? What excitement?

Very little, because there was a typhoon coming.

We werent sure how seriously to take it, but 95% of people said not to take any chances, get some food and movies together and just hibernate for the duration.

That's exactly what we did. On the day it was all kicking off (Monday), I got some alerts on my phone.

{% fig "/img/week2-alert.jpeg" "Warnings about the coming typhoon" %}

After a quick Google Translate we could see that it was a warning for elderly people to evacuate if they live in areas in danger of landslide. Nothing really for us to worry about.

As the day rolled on, and we were expecting things to really kick off... nothing much really came. It was a bit windy and rainy, but we were able to easily go out for walks with an umbrella by 8pm.

As is happens, on the Wednesday night we'd felt a little earthquake too, a 3.0 on the scale. It was very gentle, like being in a car that's being rocked.

Happy to have experienced really mild versions of both of these kinds of natural disaster, and would love for that to be it now for the whole trip!

So then today, we were free again on a nice sunny day.

I went out shopping for more things we need for the apartment, hitting the main shopping street. Several of the massive building are department stores, and they all site above the train station. The roads are very busy, so there's a set of walkways build one story up. The photo above being taken from one that spans a whole x5 way junction.
