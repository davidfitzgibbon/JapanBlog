### Shinkuku Shenanigans

We decided to have a day out in Shinkuku and do some of the touristy things we have on our list!

We started by visiting Fennec House, a cafe where you can hang out with fennec foxes!

{% fig "/img/w3-fennec-1.jpeg" "Pom - star of the show" %}

The little friends weren't that interested in being held, but Pom, the puppy of the crew, was on hand to be cuddled.

{% fig "/img/w3-fennec-2.jpeg" "Eni in her element" %}

The handlers did a really good job of trying to keep them entertained around us, but the lads weren't having it.

{% fig "/img/w3-fennec-3.jpeg" "Chillin in the corner" %}

By the end of our time, they really weren't having much of us anymore and just chilled in the corner.

### Work Chop

Next we went to a chopstick making workshop! Not only a fun bit of culture, but we could do with chopsticks, so kinda practical too!

{% fig "/img/w3-chop-1.jpeg" "Good little worker bees on the factory floor" %}

It was lots of fun really. We got to pick our wood, shave the sticks down to chopstick shape, sand them down, then oil em up!

{% fig "/img/w3-chop-2.jpeg" "Shavings and sawdust, next to the finished piece." %}

It was well worth the trip, great atmosphere in the place, definitely one to do if you're thinking of coming over!

{% fig "/img/w3-chop-3.jpeg" "Happy with our finished pieces" %}

### A trip to Kilkenny

We tried to go to Round 1, but the queues were too long, so we found the first darts bar we could and had a few games.

Then, I thought it might be fun to see what the local Irish bar, Kilkenny, was like!

{% fig "/img/w3-pint.jpeg" "A glorious creamy pint" %}

To be fair now, the pint was great! And the decor was pretty much spot on. What distracted from the effect of this hibernian elysium was that we were the only non-Japanese people in the bar, including the staff! They were all very nice, and we had a little chat or two, but we only stayed for a the one pint.

### Tsumiki Burger

It's also the harvest moon this weekend, so there have been little festivals and things around.

It's really interesting, there's lots of moon / rabbit based imagery around it. Eni tells me that there's a belief that rabbits come from the moon, because you can see a rabbit in the moon. Much like in Europe we have "the man on the moon", and the moon being made of cheese.

{% fig "/img/w3-tsumiki-3.png" "Various interpretations of the rabbit on the moon" %}

Part of this in the festival in the modern day is the Tsumiki Burger (Moon Gazing Burger). It's a nice treat to have during the festival as the weather gets a little cooler.

I did what anyone serious about learning about local culture would do.

{% fig "/img/w3-tsumiki-1.jpeg" "McDonald's Tsumiki Burger range" %}

...I went to McDonald's...

{% fig "/img/w3-tsumiki-2.jpeg" "Ah jaysus" %}

...and purchased a handful of disappointment!

Essentially a Tsumiki Burger seems to just be a burger with a perfectly round fried egg in it to represent the moon. There was way too much egg in this and it kinda clashed with some other falvour in there. Wouldn't recommend!
